\begin{proof}[Solution]
  Suppose \(av_1+bv_2=0\), where \(a,b\) are scalars. Then
  \(av_1=-bv_2\). Since \(V_1,V_2\) are subspaces,
  \[
    av_1=-bv_2\in V_1\cap V_2=\{0\}.
  \]
  Hence \(av_1=0\) and \(bv_2=0\). Both \(v_1\) and \(v_2\)
  are nonzero, so \(a=b=0\). Therefore \(\{v_1,v_2\}\) is linearly
  independent.
\end{proof}
