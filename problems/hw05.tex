\noindent\textbf{Determinant of a matrix}\quad
Let \((e_1,\ldots,e_n)\) be the standard basis of \(\mathbb F^n\).
Let \(D\) be the antisymmetric multilinear function on \(\mathbb F^n\)
such that
\[
  D(e_1,\ldots,e_n)=1.
\]
Recall that if \(c_1,\ldots,c_n\in\mathbb F^n\) are the columns of
a matrix \(M\), then the determinant of \(M\) is
\[
  \det(M)=D(c_1,\ldots,c_n).
\]
