\begin{proof}[Solution]
Let \(M\) be the matrix in the question. Its columns are
\[
  c_1=ae_1,\qquad c_2=be_1+de_2,\qquad
  c_3=ce_1+e\,e_2+fe_3.
\]
Let \(D\) be the normalized determinant function. Then
\(\det M=D(c_1,c_2,c_3)\) and \(D(e_1,e_2,e_3)=1\).
Every term with repeated basis vectors vanishes. Expanding the inputs by
multilinearity therefore gives
\begin{align*}
  \det M
    &=aD(e_1,be_1+de_2,c_3)\\
    &=abD(e_1,e_1,c_3)+adD(e_1,e_2,c_3)\\
    &=adD(e_1,e_2,ce_1+e\,e_2+fe_3)\\
    &=adcD(e_1,e_2,e_1)+adeD(e_1,e_2,e_2)\\
    &\quad+adfD(e_1,e_2,e_3)\\
    &=adf.
\end{align*}
\end{proof}
