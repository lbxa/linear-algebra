\noindent\textbf{Solution.}
Let \(A\) be a real \(n\times n\) matrix with columns \(c_1,\ldots,c_n\),
and let \(D:(\R^n)^n\to\R\) be the normalized determinant function.
The columns of \(-A\) are \(-c_1,\ldots,-c_n\). By multilinearity,
each of the \(n\) negated columns contributes a factor of \(-1\), so
\begin{align*}
  \det(-A)&=D(-c_1,\ldots,-c_n)\\
    &=(-1)^nD(c_1,\ldots,c_n)\\
    &=(-1)^n\det A.
\end{align*}
Therefore,
\[
  \det(-A)=
  \begin{cases}
    \det A & \text{if \(n\) is even},\\
    -\det A & \text{if \(n\) is odd}.
  \end{cases}
\]
\qed
