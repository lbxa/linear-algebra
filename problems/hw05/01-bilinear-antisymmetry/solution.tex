\begin{proof}[Solution]
Let \(v,w\in V\) be arbitrary. By the hypothesis and bilinearity,
\begin{align*}
  0&=A(v+w,v+w)\\
   &=A(v,v)+A(v,w)+A(w,v)+A(w,w).
\end{align*}
Since \(A(v,v)=A(w,w)=0\), this reduces to
\[
  A(v,w)+A(w,v)=0.
\]
Hence \(A(w,v)=-A(v,w)\). Since \(v,w\) were arbitrary, \(A\) is
antisymmetric.
\end{proof}
