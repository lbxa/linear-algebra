\chapter{Inner products and norms}
\label{ch:linear-algebra-i:inner-products-and-norms}

An inner product gives a vector space additional structure. It allows us
to measure lengths and angles and to speak of orthogonal vectors. The
ordinary dot product is the prototype, but the same properties apply to
spaces of polynomials, trigonometric polynomials, and matrices.

This chapter concerns real vector spaces and real inner products.
