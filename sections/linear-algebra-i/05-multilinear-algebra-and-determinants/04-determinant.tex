\section{Determinant}
\label{sec:linear-algebra-i:determinant}

% Sources: October 1 PDF, p. 30; IMG_9213. Use the column-indexed
% expansion from IMG_9213 and PDF pp. 27--28 consistently with M's columns.
% The final handwritten factor appears to repeat lower index 1. Use n,
% as in the preceding coordinate expansion and PDF pp. 27--30.
Let \((e_1,\ldots,e_n)\) be the standard basis of \(\R^n\).
By \autoref{thm:linear-algebra-i:normalized-volume-function}, there
is a unique antisymmetric multilinear function
\[
  D:(\R^n)^n\longrightarrow\R,\qquad D(e_1,\ldots,e_n)=1.
\]
For columns \(v_j=(b^1_j,\ldots,b^n_j)^T\), its formula is
\[
  \begin{aligned}
    D(v_1,\ldots,v_n)
      &=\sum_{\phi\in T_n}\varepsilon(\phi)
        b^{\phi(1)}_1\cdots b^{\phi(n)}_n\\
      &=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\varepsilon(\sigma)
        b^{\sigma(1)}_1\cdots b^{\sigma(n)}_n.
  \end{aligned}
\]

\begin{definition}
  Let \(M\) be a real \(n\)-by-\(n\) matrix with columns \(C_1,\ldots,C_n\).
  Its determinant is
  \[
    \det M=D(C_1,\ldots,C_n).
  \]
\end{definition}

Writing \(M=(b^i_j)\), with upper index \(i\) for the row and lower
index \(j\) for the column, gives the permutation formula
\begin{equation}
  \det M=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\varepsilon(\sigma)
    b^{\sigma(1)}_1\cdots b^{\sigma(n)}_n.
  \label{eq:linear-algebra-i:determinant-permutation-formula}
\end{equation}
It selects one row from each column. The equivalent row-indexed expression is
\[
  \det M=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\varepsilon(\sigma)
    b^1_{\sigma(1)}\cdots b^n_{\sigma(n)}.
\]
% PDF p. 30 uses this row-indexed form; the photos and pp. 27--28 use
% the column-indexed form. The two sums reindex by inverse permutations.
For \(n=2\), this agrees with the oriented-area formula \(ad-bc\);
for \(n=3\), it agrees with the normalized oriented volume.
