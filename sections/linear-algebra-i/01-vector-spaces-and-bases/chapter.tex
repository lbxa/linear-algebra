\chapter{Vector spaces and bases}
\label{ch:linear-algebra-i:vector-spaces-and-bases}

Linear algebra begins with two operations, addition and scalar multiplication.
Together they let us construct vectors by linear combinations. A span records
which vectors can be constructed, while a basis gives each vector a unique
description. These ideas also explain linear transformations. Such a
transformation carries a linear combination to the same combination of the
transformed vectors. Choosing bases turns that rule into a matrix calculation.
This chapter develops the language behind that connection.
