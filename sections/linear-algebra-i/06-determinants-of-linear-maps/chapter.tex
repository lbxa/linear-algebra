\chapter{Determinants of linear maps}
\label{ch:linear-algebra-i:determinants-of-linear-maps}

The determinant of a matrix describes how its linear map acts on volume
functions. Pullbacks make this description independent of a choice of basis
and explain why determinants multiply under composition.

Unless stated otherwise, the vector spaces in this chapter are finite
dimensional over \(\mathbb F=\R\) or \(\C\). The coordinate expansions
of \autoref{sec:linear-algebra-i:antisymmetric-multilinear-functions}
apply over both fields.
