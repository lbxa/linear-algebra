\chapter*{About this book}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About this book}

This book presents Lucas Barbosa's graduate Linear Algebra I and
Linear Algebra II notes in two parts. Part I covers vector spaces
and bases, matrices and vector coordinates, linear equations and maps,
kernel, image, and rank, multilinear algebra and determinants, determinants
of linear maps, eigenvalues and diagonalization, and real inner products and
their induced norms. These topics include complex eigenvalues, eigenspaces,
direct sums, orthogonality, and angles. Part II is reserved for
Linear Algebra II.

Italic links at the ends of exercises connect them to the solutions appendix,
where the full exercise statement precedes its solution. The repeated
exercise numbers link back to the main text. Solutions that have not yet
been completed are explicitly marked as pending.

Essential arguments appear in the main text. Margin notes contain secondary
observations and visual guidance.
