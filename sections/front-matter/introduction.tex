\chapter*{About this book}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About this book}

This book develops Lucas Barbosa's graduate linear algebra notes.
Part I begins with vector spaces, bases, and coordinates, then uses linear
maps to connect matrices, systems of equations, kernel, image, and rank.
Multilinear algebra leads to determinants of matrices and maps. Eigenvalues,
eigenspaces, diagonalization, and direct sums describe the structure of
endomorphisms. Real inner products then provide lengths, angles, and
orthogonality. Part II is reserved for Linear Algebra II.

Italic links at the ends of exercises lead to the solutions appendix,
where the full exercise statement precedes its solution. The repeated
exercise numbers link back to the main text. Solutions that have not yet
been completed are explicitly marked as pending.

Essential arguments appear in the body of the text. Margin notes contain secondary
observations and visual guidance.
